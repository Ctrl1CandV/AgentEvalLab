# Candidate Task Card: rsn-001 Shift Scheduling

- ID: `rsn-001-shift-scheduling`
- Primary capability: constraint reasoning
- Task: classify three independent scheduling instances as feasible, infeasible, or under-specified.
- Infeasible cases require a minimal conflict set. Feasible cases require a complete day-by-day witness. Missing parameters must be identified or handled with explicit conditional branches.
- The grader checks structured conclusions, conflict sets, and schedules. Free-form reasoning is retained for review and is not judged as hidden chain of thought.
- Offline calibration covers selected valid schedules, conflicts, and clarification cases; it is not a general measure of planning ability.
