## Instance L1-02

Staff a roster for 4 days (Day 1 to Day 4). Each day has the shifts early, late, in that order; the first shift is early and the last is late.
People: Ada, Gia, Hugo, Iris.

Rules:

- coverage: Every shift is staffed by exactly 1 different person. This rule always applies.
- apart: These pairs never work the same shift: Ada and Iris; Hugo and Iris.
- one_per_day: Nobody works more than one shift on the same day.
- rest: Nobody works the last shift of a day and the first shift of the next day.
