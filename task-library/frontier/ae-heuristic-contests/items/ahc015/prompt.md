## AHC015: Halloween Candy

The official statement is `public/statement.md`, unmodified, with its images in `public/images/`. The contest ran for 4 hours on 2022-10-30; 778 contestants are ranked in its final standings.

- **Reactive**: your program talks with the judge through standard input and output, as the statement describes; flush your output after each turn.
- Time limit: 2 s per case. Memory limit: 1024 MiB.
- Judged on the contest's 200 private cases, generated by the same tool from other seeds. As in the contest, a single case that is not accepted (wrong answer, runtime error or time limit exceeded) makes the whole submission score 0.
