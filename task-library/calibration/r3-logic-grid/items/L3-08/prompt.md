## Puzzle L3-08

There are 6 houses in a row, numbered 1 to 6 from left to right. One person lives in each house. Each attribute below takes each of its values in exactly one house.

- Name: Arlo, Bryn, Cato, Galen, Ilse, Joren
- Color: coral, jade, lilac, maroon, slate, teal
- Job: architect, courier, librarian, nurse, pilot, potter
- Fruit: apricot, fig, lychee, mango, papaya, quince
- Instrument: cello, flute, lute, oboe, trumpet, viola
- Vehicle: bicycle, canoe, kayak, moped, sedan, van

Clues:

1. The person whose Job is nurse lives directly right of the person whose Fruit is lychee.
2. The person whose Name is Ilse and the person whose Job is courier live next to each other.
3. The person whose Color is maroon and the person whose Job is potter live next to each other.
4. The person whose Instrument is flute lives directly left of the person whose Name is Galen.
5. The person whose Color is teal is the person whose Job is librarian.
6. There is exactly one house between the person whose Color is lilac and the person whose Fruit is papaya.
7. The person whose Job is courier and the person whose Vehicle is sedan live next to each other.
8. The person whose Vehicle is van is the person whose Job is architect.
9. The person whose Instrument is viola is the person whose Color is teal.
10. The person whose Instrument is cello is the person whose Job is courier.
11. There is exactly one house between the person whose Fruit is fig and the person whose Instrument is viola.
12. The person whose Color is slate is not the person whose Instrument is flute.
13. The person whose Fruit is quince lives directly left of the person whose Instrument is trumpet.
14. The person whose Vehicle is canoe is the person whose Job is courier.
15. The person whose Color is maroon lives in house 2.
16. The person whose Instrument is oboe and the person whose Color is coral live next to each other.
17. The person whose Name is Cato and the person whose Instrument is cello live next to each other.
18. The person whose Fruit is fig is the person whose Color is slate.
19. The person whose Fruit is quince lives in house 1.
20. The person whose Instrument is oboe is the person whose Vehicle is moped.
21. The person whose Job is librarian and the person whose Fruit is mango live next to each other.
22. The person whose Name is Arlo lives somewhere to the right of the person whose Vehicle is kayak.
23. There is exactly one house between the person whose Instrument is flute and the person whose Name is Joren.
24. The person whose Name is Joren is not the person whose Color is lilac.
25. The person whose Job is nurse lives directly right of the person whose Name is Cato.
