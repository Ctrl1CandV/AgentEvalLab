## Puzzle L2-08

There are 5 houses in a row, numbered 1 to 5 from left to right. One person lives in each house. Each attribute below takes each of its values in exactly one house.

- Drink: cider, cocoa, kefir, matcha, soda
- Job: baker, florist, librarian, nurse, welder
- Fruit: banana, cherry, guava, kiwi, quince
- Instrument: banjo, clarinet, harp, oboe, trumpet
- Sport: badminton, curling, handball, rugby, squash

Clues:

1. The person whose Sport is rugby lives directly right of the person whose Drink is kefir.
2. The person whose Drink is soda lives directly left of the person whose Job is baker.
3. The person whose Fruit is banana lives directly right of the person whose Instrument is trumpet.
4. The person whose Sport is handball is the person whose Fruit is quince.
5. The person whose Sport is handball and the person whose Instrument is harp live next to each other.
6. The person whose Instrument is banjo lives directly right of the person whose Job is florist.
7. The person whose Fruit is guava is the person whose Job is florist.
8. The person whose Instrument is harp does not live in house 1.
9. The person whose Job is baker is the person whose Sport is squash.
10. The person whose Drink is cocoa lives directly right of the person whose Job is florist.
11. The person whose Job is nurse is the person whose Sport is curling.
12. The person whose Sport is squash and the person whose Drink is cider live next to each other.
13. The person whose Fruit is cherry lives in house 1.
14. The person whose Job is florist is the person whose Instrument is clarinet.
15. The person whose Job is baker lives somewhere to the right of the person whose Sport is curling.
16. The person whose Drink is soda is the person whose Job is librarian.
