## Puzzle L1-03

There are 4 houses in a row, numbered 1 to 4 from left to right. One person lives in each house. Each attribute below takes each of its values in exactly one house.

- Pet: gecko, parrot, terrier, tortoise
- Hobby: birding, chess, climbing, pottery
- Fruit: apricot, banana, fig, quince
- Instrument: cello, harp, oboe, trumpet

Clues:

1. The person whose Instrument is harp is the person whose Hobby is birding.
2. The person whose Instrument is cello and the person whose Hobby is chess live next to each other.
3. The person whose Pet is terrier is the person whose Instrument is oboe.
4. The person whose Hobby is birding lives directly left of the person whose Fruit is banana.
5. There is exactly one house between the person whose Pet is gecko and the person whose Fruit is fig.
6. The person whose Hobby is climbing is the person whose Pet is tortoise.
7. The person whose Fruit is fig lives somewhere to the right of the person whose Hobby is climbing.
8. The person whose Fruit is quince lives in house 4.
9. The person whose Instrument is oboe lives directly right of the person whose Hobby is birding.
