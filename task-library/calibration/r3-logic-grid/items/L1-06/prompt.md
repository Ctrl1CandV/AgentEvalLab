## Puzzle L1-06

There are 4 houses in a row, numbered 1 to 4 from left to right. One person lives in each house. Each attribute below takes each of its values in exactly one house.

- Pet: axolotl, canary, hamster, parrot
- Drink: cider, matcha, soda, water
- Fruit: apricot, kiwi, lychee, quince
- Vehicle: bicycle, canoe, kayak, sedan

Clues:

1. The person whose Fruit is apricot is the person whose Drink is cider.
2. The person whose Drink is water lives somewhere to the right of the person whose Pet is hamster.
3. The person whose Vehicle is kayak and the person whose Fruit is apricot live next to each other.
4. The person whose Fruit is kiwi lives somewhere to the left of the person whose Pet is parrot.
5. The person whose Vehicle is canoe lives somewhere to the right of the person whose Fruit is apricot.
6. The person whose Drink is cider lives directly right of the person whose Fruit is lychee.
7. The person whose Pet is canary and the person whose Vehicle is sedan live next to each other.
8. The person whose Pet is hamster is not the person whose Vehicle is sedan.
9. The person whose Pet is axolotl is the person whose Fruit is quince.
10. The person whose Fruit is lychee is the person whose Drink is matcha.
