## Puzzle L1-01

There are 4 houses in a row, numbered 1 to 4 from left to right. One person lives in each house. Each attribute below takes each of its values in exactly one house.

- Drink: coffee, matcha, soda, water
- Instrument: banjo, clarinet, drums, flute
- Sport: badminton, curling, golf, hockey
- Vehicle: bicycle, canoe, glider, kayak

Clues:

1. The person whose Drink is coffee is the person whose Vehicle is kayak.
2. The person whose Sport is hockey lives directly left of the person whose Drink is coffee.
3. The person whose Sport is curling is the person whose Instrument is flute.
4. The person whose Vehicle is bicycle does not live in house 1.
5. The person whose Drink is water lives directly left of the person whose Sport is hockey.
6. There is exactly one house between the person whose Vehicle is bicycle and the person whose Instrument is flute.
7. The person whose Sport is hockey is the person whose Drink is soda.
8. The person whose Instrument is banjo lives directly right of the person whose Sport is golf.
9. There is exactly one house between the person whose Instrument is banjo and the person whose Vehicle is canoe.
10. The person whose Instrument is drums is the person whose Vehicle is bicycle.
