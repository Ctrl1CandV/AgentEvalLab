## Puzzle L3-02

There are 6 houses in a row, numbered 1 to 6 from left to right. One person lives in each house. Each attribute below takes each of its values in exactly one house.

- Color: cobalt, coral, ivory, lilac, ochre, slate
- Pet: axolotl, hamster, parrot, terrier, tortoise, whippet
- Job: architect, baker, librarian, nurse, pilot, potter
- Fruit: banana, cherry, fig, guava, lychee, quince
- Instrument: banjo, drums, flute, lute, oboe, trumpet
- Vehicle: bicycle, canoe, glider, scooter, tandem, tram

Clues:

1. The person whose Pet is hamster is the person whose Vehicle is canoe.
2. The person whose Vehicle is tandem lives somewhere to the right of the person whose Color is lilac.
3. The person whose Instrument is banjo lives in house 6.
4. The person whose Instrument is trumpet lives somewhere to the right of the person whose Color is ivory.
5. The person whose Fruit is guava and the person whose Pet is hamster live next to each other.
6. The person whose Instrument is oboe and the person whose Job is pilot live next to each other.
7. There is exactly one house between the person whose Color is coral and the person whose Vehicle is tram.
8. The person whose Vehicle is scooter is the person whose Color is cobalt.
9. The person whose Pet is tortoise is the person whose Vehicle is bicycle.
10. There is exactly one house between the person whose Vehicle is bicycle and the person whose Fruit is cherry.
11. The person whose Pet is hamster lives somewhere to the left of the person whose Fruit is fig.
12. The person whose Color is slate lives in house 6.
13. The person whose Color is ivory is the person whose Instrument is drums.
14. The person whose Instrument is lute lives directly left of the person whose Job is potter.
15. The person whose Pet is parrot is the person whose Fruit is banana.
16. There is exactly one house between the person whose Vehicle is scooter and the person whose Job is architect.
17. The person whose Vehicle is glider is the person whose Instrument is oboe.
18. The person whose Fruit is fig and the person whose Color is ivory live next to each other.
19. The person whose Job is potter lives somewhere to the left of the person whose Vehicle is glider.
20. The person whose Pet is axolotl is the person whose Job is librarian.
21. The person whose Vehicle is glider lives directly left of the person whose Pet is terrier.
22. The person whose Job is architect lives somewhere to the right of the person whose Fruit is banana.
23. The person whose Vehicle is bicycle lives somewhere to the left of the person whose Fruit is quince.
24. The person whose Job is nurse is the person whose Color is slate.
25. The person whose Fruit is cherry is not the person whose Vehicle is glider.
26. There is exactly one house between the person whose Vehicle is bicycle and the person whose Instrument is lute.
