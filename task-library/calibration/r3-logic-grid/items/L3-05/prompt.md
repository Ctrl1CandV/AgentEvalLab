## Puzzle L3-05

There are 6 houses in a row, numbered 1 to 6 from left to right. One person lives in each house. Each attribute below takes each of its values in exactly one house.

- Name: Bryn, Dara, Elio, Galen, Ilse, Joren
- Color: amber, cobalt, coral, lilac, ochre, teal
- Drink: cider, cocoa, coffee, kefir, soda, water
- Hobby: chess, fencing, knitting, origami, rowing, sailing
- Instrument: clarinet, flute, harp, lute, oboe, trumpet
- Sport: badminton, cricket, golf, handball, judo, polo

Clues:

2. The person whose Sport is handball is the person whose Drink is cider.
3. The person whose Instrument is lute lives directly left of the person whose Hobby is origami.

8. The person whose Hobby is sailing lives somewhere to the left of the person whose Sport is cricket.
9. The person whose Drink is coffee is the person whose Name is Galen.
10. There is exactly one house between the person whose Instrument is clarinet and the person whose Sport is polo.
11. The person whose Color is lilac is the person whose Hobby is knitting.
12. The person whose Hobby is rowing lives in house 6.
13. There is exactly one house between the person whose Hobby is sailing and the person whose Instrument is trumpet.
14. The person whose Drink is cider and the person whose Hobby is fencing live next to each other.
