## Puzzle L2-02

There are 5 houses in a row, numbered 1 to 5 from left to right. One person lives in each house. Each attribute below takes each of its values in exactly one house.

- Name: Bryn, Cato, Elio, Fenna, Joren
- Pet: canary, ferret, gecko, terrier, whippet
- Job: architect, chemist, courier, nurse, welder
- Fruit: fig, guava, kiwi, mango, quince
- Instrument: banjo, harp, lute, trumpet, viola

Clues:

1. The person whose Name is Cato lives somewhere to the right of the person whose Fruit is fig.
2. The person whose Job is welder is the person whose Instrument is trumpet.
3. There is exactly one house between the person whose Job is chemist and the person whose Pet is gecko.
4. The person whose Fruit is guava lives in house 2.
5. The person whose Name is Joren lives directly left of the person whose Instrument is trumpet.
6. The person whose Fruit is quince is the person whose Name is Cato.
7. The person whose Fruit is kiwi is the person whose Instrument is viola.
8. The person whose Instrument is harp and the person whose Job is nurse live next to each other.
9. The person whose Fruit is quince is the person whose Job is nurse.
10. The person whose Name is Fenna lives directly left of the person whose Pet is gecko.
11. The person whose Pet is ferret and the person whose Instrument is harp live next to each other.
12. The person whose Fruit is kiwi is the person whose Pet is gecko.
13. There is exactly one house between the person whose Pet is gecko and the person whose Name is Bryn.
14. The person whose Job is courier lives directly left of the person whose Pet is canary.
15. The person whose Instrument is lute is the person whose Pet is terrier.
