## Puzzle L2-06

There are 5 houses in a row, numbered 1 to 5 from left to right. One person lives in each house. Each attribute below takes each of its values in exactly one house.

- Pet: canary, gecko, parrot, rabbit, whippet
- Drink: kefir, lemonade, matcha, milk, soda
- Job: architect, baker, courier, florist, welder
- Instrument: banjo, clarinet, flute, harp, lute
- Vehicle: glider, kayak, sedan, tandem, van

Clues:

1. The person whose Drink is matcha is not the person whose Instrument is lute.
2. The person whose Vehicle is sedan is the person whose Drink is matcha.
3. The person whose Pet is canary is not the person whose Job is architect.
4. The person whose Drink is lemonade is not the person whose Job is welder.
5. The person whose Pet is whippet lives directly left of the person whose Instrument is lute.
6. The person whose Instrument is flute does not live in house 2.
7. The person whose Job is courier lives directly right of the person whose Pet is rabbit.
8. The person whose Vehicle is tandem lives directly left of the person whose Drink is lemonade.
9. The person whose Drink is milk is the person whose Vehicle is van.
10. The person whose Drink is soda lives directly left of the person whose Pet is parrot.
11. The person whose Drink is lemonade and the person whose Job is courier live next to each other.
12. The person whose Instrument is clarinet is the person whose Job is welder.
13. The person whose Pet is canary is the person whose Vehicle is sedan.
14. The person whose Pet is whippet lives directly left of the person whose Job is florist.
15. The person whose Instrument is harp lives directly right of the person whose Job is florist.
16. The person whose Job is courier lives directly right of the person whose Vehicle is kayak.
17. The person whose Job is courier and the person whose Pet is whippet live next to each other.
