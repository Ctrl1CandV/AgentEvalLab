## Puzzle L4-02

There are 7 houses in a row, numbered 1 to 7 from left to right. One person lives in each house. Each attribute below takes each of its values in exactly one house.

- Name: Arlo, Bryn, Cato, Elio, Fenna, Galen, Hollis
- Pet: axolotl, canary, gecko, hamster, parrot, rabbit, whippet
- Job: baker, courier, florist, librarian, nurse, pilot, welder
- Fruit: apricot, banana, cherry, fig, guava, kiwi, lychee
- Instrument: banjo, cello, clarinet, flute, harp, lute, viola
- Sport: badminton, cricket, handball, hockey, polo, rugby, squash
- Vehicle: bicycle, glider, moped, scooter, sedan, tandem, van

3. The person whose Pet is hamster lives somewhere to the right of the person whose Sport is handball.

9. The person whose Sport is polo is the person whose Job is florist.
