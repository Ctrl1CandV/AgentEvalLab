## Puzzle L1-08

There are 4 houses in a row, numbered 1 to 4 from left to right. One person lives in each house. Each attribute below takes each of its values in exactly one house.

- Drink: cocoa, matcha, milk, soda
- Hobby: archery, birding, fencing, pottery
- Instrument: banjo, clarinet, drums, flute
- Sport: badminton, cricket, handball, hockey

Clues:

1. The person whose Sport is badminton lives directly right of the person whose Hobby is pottery.
2. There is exactly one house between the person whose Instrument is clarinet and the person whose Drink is cocoa.
3. There is exactly one house between the person whose Drink is soda and the person whose Instrument is drums.
4. There is exactly one house between the person whose Drink is soda and the person whose Hobby is birding.
5. There is exactly one house between the person whose Sport is hockey and the person whose Hobby is pottery.
6. The person whose Instrument is flute lives somewhere to the left of the person whose Sport is handball.
7. The person whose Hobby is pottery does not live in house 3.
8. The person whose Drink is cocoa lives directly left of the person whose Hobby is archery.
9. The person whose Drink is milk lives directly right of the person whose Instrument is banjo.
