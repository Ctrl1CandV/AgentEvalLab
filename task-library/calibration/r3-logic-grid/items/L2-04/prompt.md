## Puzzle L2-04

There are 5 houses in a row, numbered 1 to 5 from left to right. One person lives in each house. Each attribute below takes each of its values in exactly one house.

- Name: Bryn, Dara, Fenna, Galen, Joren
- Drink: coffee, kefir, milk, oolong, water
- Job: baker, chemist, librarian, nurse, potter
- Hobby: archery, birding, knitting, origami, pottery
- Fruit: cherry, fig, guava, kiwi, papaya

Clues:

1. The person whose Drink is water is the person whose Job is librarian.
2. The person whose Fruit is cherry lives directly right of the person whose Job is nurse.
3. The person whose Job is baker lives somewhere to the left of the person whose Fruit is papaya.
4. The person whose Drink is water lives in house 2.
5. The person whose Hobby is origami lives somewhere to the right of the person whose Name is Bryn.
6. The person whose Hobby is birding lives somewhere to the right of the person whose Drink is coffee.
7. The person whose Job is chemist does not live in house 1.
8. The person whose Fruit is fig is the person whose Job is chemist.
9. The person whose Hobby is birding lives directly left of the person whose Job is potter.
10. The person whose Fruit is kiwi is not the person whose Name is Galen.
11. The person whose Name is Dara is the person whose Drink is milk.
12. The person whose Drink is milk lives directly right of the person whose Hobby is knitting.
13. There is exactly one house between the person whose Fruit is guava and the person whose Job is potter.
14. The person whose Drink is kefir lives somewhere to the left of the person whose Job is potter.
15. The person whose Drink is kefir does not live in house 1.
16. The person whose Name is Bryn lives in house 3.
17. There is exactly one house between the person whose Job is baker and the person whose Hobby is pottery.
18. The person whose Hobby is knitting and the person whose Name is Fenna live next to each other.
