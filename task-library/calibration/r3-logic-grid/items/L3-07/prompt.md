## Puzzle L3-07

There are 6 houses in a row, numbered 1 to 6 from left to right. One person lives in each house. Each attribute below takes each of its values in exactly one house.

- Color: coral, ivory, jade, lilac, ochre, slate
- Pet: axolotl, ferret, gecko, parrot, rabbit, tortoise
- Drink: cider, coffee, kefir, matcha, soda, water
- Hobby: birding, chess, fencing, knitting, rowing, sailing
- Fruit: apricot, cherry, fig, kiwi, mango, quince
- Vehicle: canoe, glider, moped, scooter, tandem, tram

Clues:

1. There is exactly one house between the person whose Hobby is knitting and the person whose Color is ochre.
2. The person whose Pet is ferret is the person whose Color is ochre.
3. The person whose Color is slate is the person whose Drink is kefir.
4. The person whose Drink is water is the person whose Vehicle is glider.
5. There is exactly one house between the person whose Pet is tortoise and the person whose Hobby is knitting.
6. The person whose Fruit is cherry is the person whose Vehicle is tram.
7. The person whose Fruit is apricot is the person whose Pet is axolotl.
8. The person whose Vehicle is scooter lives directly right of the person whose Fruit is kiwi.
9. The person whose Vehicle is tandem and the person whose Pet is gecko live next to each other.
10. The person whose Vehicle is tram is the person whose Drink is matcha.
11. The person whose Hobby is chess lives in house 6.
12. The person whose Hobby is rowing is the person whose Pet is parrot.
13. The person whose Color is jade is the person whose Drink is water.
14. The person whose Color is slate and the person whose Vehicle is tram live next to each other.
15. The person whose Pet is axolotl lives directly right of the person whose Hobby is birding.
16. The person whose Color is lilac lives directly right of the person whose Fruit is fig.
17. The person whose Vehicle is moped lives in house 1.
18. There is exactly one house between the person whose Drink is soda and the person whose Fruit is quince.
19. There is exactly one house between the person whose Vehicle is canoe and the person whose Drink is coffee.
20. The person whose Vehicle is tram lives directly left of the person whose Color is ochre.
21. The person whose Hobby is sailing lives somewhere to the left of the person whose Fruit is fig.
22. The person whose Pet is axolotl is the person whose Color is coral.
