# Calibration Record: T2 Finance Desk

T2's second package, after `t2-service-desk`.

**Why.** On 2026-10-22 the 0.4.0 shifts of `t2-service-desk` put deepseek-flash and qwen3.8-flash at 100%. Each rule's conditions sat on the record being handled, and each computation was a step or two. The user asked for a substantially harder T2 in a corporate-finance setting, with more complex data and harder requirements. Benchmark failure analyses point to where agents lose points:

- τ-bench: about 55% of failures are wrong arguments from numerical reasoning over complex databases; compound requests are another main source.
- τ²-bench: conditional multi-step procedures; scores fall as the number of actions grows.

## Version 0.1.0 (2026-10-22)

- **Items.** `fn1`–`fn4`, one close day each: books, ten requests (see the rubric), `ledger.py` and `MANUAL.md`. Generated by `private/finance.py` and built by `private/build_package.py` into `private/days.json`.
- **Selection.** A day is kept only when:
  - each planted point holds, for example a shortfall within 5.00 or a variance within 2% but above 50.00;
  - the vendor invoices end as planted (rejected, posted, blocked, posted or held);
  - at least five of six modelled misreadings change some request: oldest-first allocation, no discount, no write-off, the conversion cost booked as exchange result, posting into the closed period, revaluing today's open items.
- **Grading** (grader 1.0.0): per request against the oracle on the request's starting state, as the rubric describes.

**Self-calibration** (`python3 private/grader.py --self-calibrate`; `logs/self-calibration-0.1.0.log`):

- **Ten hand-worked examples**, each computed by hand from the manual and compared with the oracle:
  - a discount of 24.20 on 1,210.00, of which 4.20 is VAT;
  - 1,000.00 USD carried at 900.00 and worth 920.00, with a 2.00 conversion cost;
  - a settlement at 0.912345 rounding to 45.62, then 45.61;
  - oldest first ending in a write-off of 3.00;
  - an exact match by alias;
  - reverse charge;
  - the closed AP period;
  - number normalisation;
  - a revaluation that ignores an October receipt;
  - a day count of 15 days out of 30.
- **On every day:**
  - the oracle's commands through `ledger.py` itself pass every check, and so do they with a reply after prose, and without begin markers;
  - the untouched books and a reply alone score 0;
  - a desk that slips once, misbooking a receipt or holding a duplicate, and then handles every later request right on its own books, loses only that request's checks;
  - each modelled misreading loses the checks of the requests it touches: 6–26% of the day.
