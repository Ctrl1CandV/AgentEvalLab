## Instance L2-08

Staff a roster for 5 days (Day 1 to Day 5). Each day has the shifts early, late, in that order; the first shift is early and the last is late.

Rules:

- max_shifts: Nobody works more than 2 shifts in total.

- rest: Nobody works the last shift of a day and the first shift of the next day.
- senior: Every shift includes at least one senior person (Hugo).
