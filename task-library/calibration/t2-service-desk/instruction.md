# Task: Service Desk (T2)

You work the service desk of a small online shop. Everything is in the directory `public/` of the workspace: the shop's policy, `POLICY.md`; its data, in `data/`; and its desk tool, `desk.py`. Run commands from inside `public/`, for example `python3 desk.py show-order O-5001`.

Handle the customer ticket below exactly as the policy says. Carry out what the policy allows and decline the rest, change the shop's data only through `desk.py`, and end with the JSON reply that `POLICY.md` describes.
