# Task: Stockroom (AP)

Run a store's stockroom for 40 days and make as much profit as you can. The rules, the demand model, the suppliers and the score are in `public/RULES.md`; read them first. You act only through the `simulator` tool (`observe`, `order`, `advance`, `result`); you may also write and run programs in the workspace to plan, but orders go through the tool.

End day 40 yourself: a run that stops earlier does not count. Then reply with a short summary of your policy and the final result.
