## Puzzle L2-05

There are 5 houses in a row, numbered 1 to 5 from left to right. One person lives in each house. Each attribute below takes each of its values in exactly one house.

- Name: Bryn, Dara, Elio, Ilse, Joren
- Pet: hamster, parrot, terrier, tortoise, whippet
- Hobby: archery, birding, climbing, fencing, pottery
- Sport: curling, golf, hockey, judo, squash
- Vehicle: bicycle, kayak, moped, scooter, sedan

Clues:

1. The person whose Pet is parrot lives directly left of the person whose Sport is squash.
2. There is exactly one house between the person whose Name is Elio and the person whose Vehicle is bicycle.
3. The person whose Hobby is birding is the person whose Sport is squash.
4. The person whose Name is Joren and the person whose Sport is golf live next to each other.
5. The person whose Hobby is birding lives directly left of the person whose Sport is judo.
6. The person whose Hobby is climbing is not the person whose Sport is hockey.
7. The person whose Vehicle is moped is the person whose Sport is hockey.
8. There is exactly one house between the person whose Name is Elio and the person whose Pet is terrier.
9. There is exactly one house between the person whose Pet is hamster and the person whose Name is Dara.
10. The person whose Hobby is archery lives directly left of the person whose Sport is curling.
11. The person whose Sport is squash and the person whose Pet is whippet live next to each other.
12. The person whose Vehicle is sedan lives directly left of the person whose Name is Bryn.
13. The person whose Vehicle is scooter is the person whose Pet is hamster.
14. The person whose Name is Ilse lives somewhere to the left of the person whose Sport is hockey.
15. The person whose Sport is golf is the person whose Hobby is fencing.
