## Puzzle L1-05

There are 4 houses in a row, numbered 1 to 4 from left to right. One person lives in each house. Each attribute below takes each of its values in exactly one house.

- Name: Arlo, Bryn, Elio, Galen
- Color: maroon, ochre, slate, teal
- Hobby: birding, fencing, knitting, sailing
- Fruit: apricot, fig, guava, papaya

Clues:

1. The person whose Name is Arlo is the person whose Color is ochre.
2. The person whose Fruit is papaya is the person whose Hobby is fencing.
3. The person whose Color is ochre does not live in house 3.
4. There is exactly one house between the person whose Fruit is papaya and the person whose Hobby is sailing.
5. The person whose Fruit is fig lives somewhere to the left of the person whose Color is teal.
6. The person whose Name is Elio lives directly left of the person whose Color is ochre.
7. The person whose Color is slate lives directly left of the person whose Hobby is knitting.
8. The person whose Fruit is guava is not the person whose Name is Bryn.
9. The person whose Name is Arlo lives directly right of the person whose Color is maroon.
10. The person whose Fruit is apricot is the person whose Name is Galen.
11. The person whose Color is ochre is not the person whose Fruit is guava.
