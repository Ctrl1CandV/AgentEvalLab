## Puzzle L3-03

There are 6 houses in a row, numbered 1 to 6 from left to right. One person lives in each house. Each attribute below takes each of its values in exactly one house.

- Name: Bryn, Cato, Dara, Galen, Hollis, Ilse
- Color: amber, coral, ivory, maroon, slate, teal
- Drink: cider, coffee, lemonade, matcha, milk, soda
- Hobby: archery, birding, chess, fencing, origami, pottery
- Instrument: clarinet, flute, harp, lute, oboe, trumpet
- Vehicle: canoe, kayak, moped, scooter, tram, van

Clues:

1. The person whose Instrument is trumpet is not the person whose Hobby is chess.
2. The person whose Name is Hollis lives in house 5.
3. The person whose Drink is milk lives directly right of the person whose Hobby is chess.
4. The person whose Hobby is archery lives directly left of the person whose Name is Bryn.
5. The person whose Instrument is clarinet is the person whose Name is Cato.
6. The person whose Hobby is pottery is the person whose Vehicle is canoe.
7. There is exactly one house between the person whose Vehicle is canoe and the person whose Drink is cider.
8. There is exactly one house between the person whose Name is Galen and the person whose Instrument is flute.
9. The person whose Drink is matcha is the person whose Instrument is flute.
10. The person whose Name is Galen lives in house 3.
11. The person whose Drink is lemonade lives somewhere to the left of the person whose Instrument is oboe.
12. The person whose Instrument is oboe is the person whose Drink is milk.
13. The person whose Hobby is origami lives somewhere to the left of the person whose Color is maroon.
14. The person whose Name is Dara and the person whose Instrument is oboe live next to each other.
15. The person whose Drink is coffee lives somewhere to the right of the person whose Instrument is harp.
16. The person whose Hobby is archery and the person whose Color is teal live next to each other.
17. The person whose Vehicle is kayak is the person whose Instrument is harp.
18. The person whose Drink is soda lives in house 2.
19. The person whose Vehicle is moped lives in house 1.
20. The person whose Hobby is origami lives directly left of the person whose Vehicle is scooter.
21. The person whose Instrument is trumpet lives directly left of the person whose Hobby is fencing.
22. The person whose Color is slate is the person whose Instrument is trumpet.
23. The person whose Color is amber is the person whose Vehicle is tram.
24. There is exactly one house between the person whose Color is coral and the person whose Instrument is clarinet.
