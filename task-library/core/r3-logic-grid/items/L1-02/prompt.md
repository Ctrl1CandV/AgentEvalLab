## Puzzle L1-02

There are 4 houses in a row, numbered 1 to 4 from left to right. One person lives in each house. Each attribute below takes each of its values in exactly one house.

- Color: coral, ivory, ochre, teal
- Drink: cider, coffee, lemonade, water
- Hobby: archery, chess, fencing, knitting
- Vehicle: canoe, kayak, scooter, van

Clues:

1. The person whose Hobby is archery is the person whose Color is ochre.
2. The person whose Color is ivory is the person whose Hobby is fencing.
3. The person whose Vehicle is canoe lives directly right of the person whose Color is coral.
4. The person whose Color is coral lives somewhere to the right of the person whose Vehicle is van.
5. The person whose Color is ivory lives directly right of the person whose Drink is lemonade.
6. The person whose Vehicle is kayak lives directly right of the person whose Drink is cider.
7. The person whose Drink is coffee lives directly left of the person whose Color is coral.
8. The person whose Hobby is knitting lives directly left of the person whose Vehicle is kayak.
