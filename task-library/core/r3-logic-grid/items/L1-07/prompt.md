## Puzzle L1-07

There are 4 houses in a row, numbered 1 to 4 from left to right. One person lives in each house. Each attribute below takes each of its values in exactly one house.

- Name: Bryn, Fenna, Galen, Hollis
- Job: librarian, nurse, potter, welder
- Hobby: climbing, origami, rowing, sailing
- Instrument: banjo, clarinet, drums, flute

Clues:

1. The person whose Hobby is rowing is the person whose Name is Bryn.
2. The person whose Job is librarian lives directly right of the person whose Hobby is sailing.
3. The person whose Instrument is drums does not live in house 3.
4. The person whose Job is nurse lives somewhere to the left of the person whose Name is Hollis.
5. There is exactly one house between the person whose Instrument is clarinet and the person whose Name is Fenna.
6. There is exactly one house between the person whose Job is librarian and the person whose Hobby is climbing.
7. The person whose Name is Fenna and the person whose Job is potter live next to each other.
8. The person whose Job is nurse lives in house 1.
9. The person whose Job is nurse lives directly left of the person whose Instrument is banjo.
10. The person whose Name is Bryn is the person whose Instrument is clarinet.
11. The person whose Hobby is origami lives somewhere to the left of the person whose Name is Bryn.
