## Puzzle P8x7-01

- Fruit: apricot, banana, fig, guava, lychee, mango, papaya, quince
- Instrument: cello, clarinet, drums, flute, harp, lute, oboe, trumpet
- Sport: badminton, cricket, curling, golf, handball, hockey, judo, polo
