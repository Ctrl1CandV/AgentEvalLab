## Puzzle L2-03

There are 5 houses in a row, numbered 1 to 5 from left to right. One person lives in each house. Each attribute below takes each of its values in exactly one house.

- Job: baker, florist, librarian, nurse, welder
- Hobby: birding, fencing, knitting, origami, pottery
- Fruit: banana, cherry, fig, kiwi, mango
- Instrument: banjo, cello, drums, flute, trumpet
- Vehicle: canoe, moped, scooter, sedan, tram

Clues:

1. The person whose Job is nurse and the person whose Fruit is mango live next to each other.
2. The person whose Fruit is mango is not the person whose Vehicle is canoe.
3. The person whose Vehicle is tram and the person whose Instrument is flute live next to each other.
4. The person whose Hobby is birding and the person whose Instrument is cello live next to each other.
5. The person whose Vehicle is sedan lives directly right of the person whose Hobby is pottery.
6. There is exactly one house between the person whose Hobby is birding and the person whose Job is librarian.
7. The person whose Instrument is trumpet and the person whose Hobby is fencing live next to each other.
8. The person whose Instrument is banjo does not live in house 1.
9. There is exactly one house between the person whose Hobby is birding and the person whose Job is florist.
10. The person whose Job is librarian does not live in house 5.
11. The person whose Vehicle is canoe lives in house 3.
12. The person whose Job is nurse and the person whose Fruit is banana live next to each other.
13. There is exactly one house between the person whose Instrument is flute and the person whose Hobby is origami.
14. The person whose Fruit is fig is the person whose Instrument is trumpet.
15. The person whose Job is nurse is the person whose Fruit is fig.
16. The person whose Job is baker and the person whose Fruit is cherry live next to each other.
17. The person whose Fruit is fig does not live in house 2.
18. There is exactly one house between the person whose Vehicle is moped and the person whose Fruit is cherry.
