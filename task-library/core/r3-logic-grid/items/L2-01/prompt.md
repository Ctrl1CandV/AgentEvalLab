## Puzzle L2-01

There are 5 houses in a row, numbered 1 to 5 from left to right. One person lives in each house. Each attribute below takes each of its values in exactly one house.

- Name: Bryn, Dara, Elio, Ilse, Joren
- Color: amber, cobalt, lilac, slate, teal
- Pet: hamster, parrot, rabbit, terrier, whippet
- Drink: cider, kefir, lemonade, matcha, milk
- Sport: badminton, cricket, curling, handball, polo

Clues:

1. The person whose Pet is terrier does not live in house 5.
2. The person whose Drink is milk lives directly left of the person whose Sport is curling.
3. The person whose Drink is milk is the person whose Name is Ilse.
4. The person whose Pet is parrot lives directly left of the person whose Color is lilac.
5. There is exactly one house between the person whose Color is slate and the person whose Sport is handball.
6. The person whose Color is cobalt lives directly right of the person whose Pet is rabbit.
7. The person whose Color is lilac and the person whose Drink is milk live next to each other.
8. The person whose Drink is lemonade lives in house 5.
9. The person whose Sport is curling lives somewhere to the right of the person whose Drink is matcha.
10. The person whose Sport is curling lives directly right of the person whose Color is teal.
11. The person whose Drink is cider lives somewhere to the left of the person whose Color is lilac.
12. The person whose Name is Bryn lives in house 1.
13. The person whose Pet is whippet and the person whose Drink is cider live next to each other.
14. The person whose Sport is cricket is the person whose Name is Elio.
15. The person whose Sport is polo is the person whose Name is Joren.
16. The person whose Name is Ilse and the person whose Drink is cider live next to each other.
17. The person whose Sport is curling lives somewhere to the left of the person whose Name is Elio.
