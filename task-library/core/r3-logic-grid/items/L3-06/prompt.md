## Puzzle L3-06

There are 6 houses in a row, numbered 1 to 6 from left to right. One person lives in each house. Each attribute below takes each of its values in exactly one house.

- Name: Arlo, Cato, Dara, Fenna, Galen, Ilse
- Drink: cider, cocoa, lemonade, matcha, oolong, soda
- Job: architect, baker, chemist, courier, florist, welder
- Instrument: banjo, flute, harp, lute, oboe, viola
- Sport: badminton, cricket, curling, golf, handball, polo
- Vehicle: bicycle, canoe, glider, sedan, tandem, van

Clues:

1. The person whose Instrument is lute is the person whose Drink is cocoa.
2. The person whose Vehicle is tandem lives directly left of the person whose Sport is polo.
3. The person whose Name is Cato lives directly left of the person whose Job is courier.
4. The person whose Drink is oolong lives directly left of the person whose Vehicle is tandem.
5. There is exactly one house between the person whose Sport is curling and the person whose Name is Galen.
6. The person whose Instrument is harp is the person whose Job is welder.
7. The person whose Name is Dara does not live in house 2.
8. The person whose Name is Arlo lives in house 3.
9. There is exactly one house between the person whose Drink is cider and the person whose Instrument is banjo.
10. The person whose Instrument is banjo and the person whose Name is Fenna live next to each other.
11. The person whose Job is architect is the person whose Vehicle is glider.
12. The person whose Instrument is oboe lives in house 1.
13. The person whose Vehicle is bicycle is the person whose Drink is matcha.
14. The person whose Job is chemist does not live in house 1.
15. The person whose Instrument is viola is the person whose Vehicle is tandem.
16. There is exactly one house between the person whose Name is Fenna and the person whose Vehicle is sedan.
17. There is exactly one house between the person whose Drink is cider and the person whose Sport is cricket.
18. The person whose Vehicle is bicycle lives somewhere to the left of the person whose Instrument is viola.
19. The person whose Vehicle is van is the person whose Sport is handball.
20. There is exactly one house between the person whose Sport is badminton and the person whose Drink is soda.
21. The person whose Instrument is flute is the person whose Sport is handball.
22. There is exactly one house between the person whose Vehicle is tandem and the person whose Sport is badminton.
23. The person whose Vehicle is van is the person whose Job is florist.
24. The person whose Drink is cocoa is the person whose Name is Dara.
