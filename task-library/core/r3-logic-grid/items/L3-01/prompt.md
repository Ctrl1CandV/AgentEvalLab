## Puzzle L3-01

There are 6 houses in a row, numbered 1 to 6 from left to right. One person lives in each house. Each attribute below takes each of its values in exactly one house.

- Pet: axolotl, ferret, gecko, parrot, terrier, whippet
- Drink: coffee, kefir, matcha, milk, oolong, water
- Job: baker, chemist, librarian, nurse, potter, welder
- Fruit: cherry, fig, guava, lychee, papaya, quince
- Sport: curling, golf, handball, hockey, polo, squash
- Vehicle: canoe, glider, moped, scooter, tandem, van

Clues:

1. The person whose Pet is axolotl is the person whose Sport is hockey.
2. The person whose Fruit is quince is the person whose Pet is ferret.
3. There is exactly one house between the person whose Job is baker and the person whose Drink is coffee.
4. The person whose Sport is handball lives somewhere to the left of the person whose Pet is terrier.
5. The person whose Sport is squash lives in house 5.
6. The person whose Fruit is cherry lives somewhere to the right of the person whose Vehicle is van.
7. The person whose Vehicle is van is not the person whose Pet is whippet.
8. The person whose Sport is curling lives directly right of the person whose Fruit is papaya.
9. The person whose Fruit is fig and the person whose Job is librarian live next to each other.
10. The person whose Fruit is fig and the person whose Drink is kefir live next to each other.
11. The person whose Fruit is guava and the person whose Drink is water live next to each other.
12. The person whose Sport is hockey lives directly right of the person whose Job is librarian.
13. The person whose Drink is milk is the person whose Job is chemist.
14. The person whose Pet is axolotl is the person whose Job is chemist.
15. The person whose Vehicle is tandem lives somewhere to the right of the person whose Job is potter.
16. The person whose Vehicle is van and the person whose Pet is parrot live next to each other.
17. The person whose Drink is water and the person whose Sport is curling live next to each other.
18. The person whose Vehicle is canoe lives in house 6.
19. The person whose Sport is polo and the person whose Vehicle is van live next to each other.
20. The person whose Job is baker does not live in house 6.
21. The person whose Drink is water lives in house 5.
22. The person whose Vehicle is scooter lives somewhere to the left of the person whose Job is chemist.
23. The person whose Pet is parrot lives directly left of the person whose Job is nurse.
24. The person whose Vehicle is glider is the person whose Fruit is quince.
25. The person whose Fruit is quince and the person whose Drink is oolong live next to each other.
26. There is exactly one house between the person whose Job is baker and the person whose Pet is parrot.
27. There is exactly one house between the person whose Sport is golf and the person whose Job is baker.
