## Puzzle L4-08

There are 7 houses in a row, numbered 1 to 7 from left to right. One person lives in each house. Each attribute below takes each of its values in exactly one house.

- Color: amber, cobalt, ivory, lilac, maroon, ochre, slate
- Pet: axolotl, canary, ferret, gecko, rabbit, terrier, tortoise
- Drink: cider, coffee, lemonade, matcha, milk, oolong, water
- Job: chemist, courier, librarian, nurse, pilot, potter, welder
- Fruit: apricot, banana, cherry, fig, guava, lychee, quince
- Instrument: banjo, cello, clarinet, drums, harp, lute, viola
- Vehicle: bicycle, canoe, kayak, moped, scooter, tandem, van

Clues:

1. The person whose Vehicle is scooter and the person whose Instrument is cello live next to each other.
2. The person whose Instrument is drums and the person whose Fruit is quince live next to each other.
3. The person whose Instrument is clarinet is not the person whose Job is pilot.
4. The person whose Pet is terrier lives somewhere to the left of the person whose Instrument is drums.
5. The person whose Pet is terrier is the person whose Instrument is cello.
6. The person whose Job is nurse lives somewhere to the right of the person whose Instrument is cello.
7. The person whose Color is cobalt lives somewhere to the left of the person whose Job is welder.
8. The person whose Pet is gecko is the person whose Drink is oolong.
9. The person whose Pet is canary lives in house 7.
10. There is exactly one house between the person whose Drink is coffee and the person whose Color is lilac.
11. The person whose Color is maroon and the person whose Job is librarian live next to each other.
12. The person whose Instrument is cello is the person whose Drink is water.
13. The person whose Vehicle is canoe is the person whose Fruit is quince.
14. The person whose Pet is tortoise and the person whose Fruit is guava live next to each other.
15. The person whose Instrument is viola lives in house 5.
16. The person whose Pet is rabbit lives in house 5.
17. There is exactly one house between the person whose Drink is lemonade and the person whose Pet is tortoise.
18. The person whose Color is ochre lives directly left of the person whose Job is potter.
19. The person whose Color is ochre is the person whose Job is courier.
20. The person whose Drink is coffee is the person whose Color is amber.
21. The person whose Color is ivory lives directly right of the person whose Pet is ferret.
22. The person whose Drink is cider lives somewhere to the right of the person whose Instrument is lute.
23. There is exactly one house between the person whose Job is welder and the person whose Instrument is lute.
24. The person whose Pet is rabbit is not the person whose Vehicle is kayak.

30. The person whose Fruit is lychee and the person whose Drink is milk live next to each other.
31. The person whose Fruit is banana is the person whose Vehicle is moped.
32. The person whose Drink is milk is the person whose Vehicle is kayak.

35. The person whose Vehicle is canoe is the person whose Job is welder.
36. There is exactly one house between the person whose Color is ivory and the person whose Drink is cider.
