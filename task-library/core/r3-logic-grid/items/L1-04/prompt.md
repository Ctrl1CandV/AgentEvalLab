## Puzzle L1-04

There are 4 houses in a row, numbered 1 to 4 from left to right. One person lives in each house. Each attribute below takes each of its values in exactly one house.

- Name: Bryn, Dara, Fenna, Galen
- Hobby: archery, fencing, knitting, origami
- Fruit: apricot, banana, guava, kiwi
- Vehicle: bicycle, glider, scooter, tandem

Clues:

1. The person whose Fruit is guava lives in house 2.
2. There is exactly one house between the person whose Hobby is archery and the person whose Name is Galen.
3. The person whose Name is Fenna lives somewhere to the left of the person whose Hobby is origami.
4. There is exactly one house between the person whose Vehicle is tandem and the person whose Name is Galen.
5. The person whose Vehicle is scooter is the person whose Name is Fenna.
6. The person whose Fruit is kiwi is the person whose Hobby is fencing.
7. The person whose Fruit is kiwi lives in house 3.
8. The person whose Hobby is fencing lives somewhere to the right of the person whose Vehicle is glider.
9. The person whose Fruit is apricot is the person whose Name is Galen.
10. The person whose Vehicle is tandem is the person whose Name is Bryn.
