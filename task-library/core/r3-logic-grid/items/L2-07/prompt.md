## Puzzle L2-07

There are 5 houses in a row, numbered 1 to 5 from left to right. One person lives in each house. Each attribute below takes each of its values in exactly one house.

- Color: amber, ivory, maroon, ochre, slate
- Pet: hamster, parrot, rabbit, terrier, whippet
- Hobby: archery, chess, climbing, origami, rowing
- Fruit: banana, guava, mango, papaya, quince
- Instrument: banjo, flute, lute, oboe, viola

Clues:

1. The person whose Instrument is lute is the person whose Hobby is archery.
2. The person whose Color is slate lives directly right of the person whose Instrument is flute.
3. The person whose Hobby is climbing lives directly right of the person whose Instrument is viola.
4. The person whose Hobby is chess is the person whose Fruit is guava.
5. The person whose Pet is terrier lives directly left of the person whose Fruit is papaya.
6. The person whose Fruit is papaya is not the person whose Color is slate.
7. The person whose Color is ochre is the person whose Pet is parrot.
8. The person whose Color is maroon lives directly left of the person whose Instrument is banjo.
9. The person whose Fruit is guava lives in house 4.
10. The person whose Color is maroon is the person whose Pet is hamster.
11. The person whose Hobby is climbing lives somewhere to the left of the person whose Fruit is mango.
12. The person whose Color is slate lives directly left of the person whose Fruit is quince.
13. The person whose Color is amber lives somewhere to the right of the person whose Instrument is banjo.
14. The person whose Pet is whippet lives directly right of the person whose Hobby is rowing.
