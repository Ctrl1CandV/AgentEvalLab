## Puzzle L3-04

There are 6 houses in a row, numbered 1 to 6 from left to right. One person lives in each house. Each attribute below takes each of its values in exactly one house.

- Pet: axolotl, gecko, parrot, terrier, tortoise, whippet
- Drink: cider, coffee, kefir, lemonade, matcha, oolong
- Hobby: birding, chess, climbing, knitting, origami, pottery
- Instrument: cello, clarinet, flute, lute, oboe, viola
- Sport: cricket, curling, golf, handball, hockey, squash
- Vehicle: bicycle, kayak, moped, sedan, tandem, tram

Clues:

1. The person whose Pet is tortoise lives somewhere to the right of the person whose Vehicle is kayak.
2. The person whose Vehicle is bicycle lives somewhere to the left of the person whose Drink is lemonade.
3. The person whose Instrument is cello is not the person whose Hobby is knitting.
4. The person whose Vehicle is tram is the person whose Sport is golf.
5. There is exactly one house between the person whose Vehicle is sedan and the person whose Drink is cider.
6. The person whose Hobby is origami is the person whose Drink is lemonade.
7. The person whose Pet is gecko is the person whose Instrument is oboe.
8. The person whose Hobby is chess is the person whose Sport is curling.
9. The person whose Sport is hockey lives somewhere to the left of the person whose Pet is parrot.
10. The person whose Drink is coffee is the person whose Sport is hockey.
11. The person whose Pet is axolotl lives directly left of the person whose Instrument is oboe.
12. The person whose Hobby is birding is the person whose Vehicle is moped.
13. The person whose Pet is tortoise and the person whose Instrument is lute live next to each other.
14. There is exactly one house between the person whose Drink is matcha and the person whose Instrument is oboe.
15. There is exactly one house between the person whose Drink is kefir and the person whose Instrument is viola.
16. The person whose Pet is axolotl lives directly right of the person whose Sport is squash.
17. The person whose Drink is cider and the person whose Pet is terrier live next to each other.
18. The person whose Sport is hockey lives directly right of the person whose Hobby is chess.
19. The person whose Sport is curling is the person whose Instrument is oboe.
20. The person whose Pet is axolotl is the person whose Hobby is pottery.
21. There is exactly one house between the person whose Vehicle is tram and the person whose Instrument is cello.
22. The person whose Instrument is lute lives directly right of the person whose Vehicle is kayak.
23. The person whose Sport is curling lives somewhere to the left of the person whose Instrument is flute.
24. The person whose Instrument is flute is the person whose Sport is cricket.
25. The person whose Drink is matcha is not the person whose Vehicle is moped.
