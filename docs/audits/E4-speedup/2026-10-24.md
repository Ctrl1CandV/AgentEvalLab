## Resolution (2026-10-24)

- **Three false passes, fixed by grader 1.5.0** (commit `e15db23`). Pinned down one input class at a time against the original:
  - deepseek-flash, 0.5.0 (`d36a3fab`): rows without the referrer column, which the original reads with referrer "-";
  - qwen3.8-flash, 0.5.0 (`f5517516`, 8/8): a literal "-" referrer, which the original leaves out of the transitions;
  - qwen3.8-flash, 0.4.0 (`8e559383`): numbers in full-width or Arabic-Indic digits, which `int()` reads.
  The task promises byte-identical output "for every input", so each is a defect inside the contract. The hidden quirks day now holds these rows; the small and large days, the public sample and every item file are unchanged; three new mutants fail `quirks` only.
- **Re-grade** of every 0.5.0 E4 trial (speedup and compress, all three models): deepseek's speedup 87.5% to 75.0% (`quirks`), qwen's 100% to 75.0% (`quirks` and `speed-0.75`), mimo's unchanged at 87.5%, compress unchanged. The 0.4.x trials belong to superseded task versions and keep their graders' numbers; this record is their audit.
- **Open: the speed rungs are noisy.** qwen's `speed-0.75` flipped on re-grading with no change to the program. Four repeated runs of the hidden check on its delivery gave time ratios of 0.753, 0.740, 0.781 and 0.770 against the reference: the 0.75 rung passes or fails by chance, and a 3% timing spread becomes a 12.5% score step. This fails the noise gate (VALIDATION §1). E4 speedup goes to the basic tier in v2, where the time ratio is reported as a raw value and rungs are labels only (MEASUREMENT §3); the measurement moves to CPU time with its card (PLAN-007 WP2.2).
