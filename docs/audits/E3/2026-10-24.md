Grader 2.3.0 (commit `e15db23`) gives commands no brief bounds in time 30 seconds, retries a sandbox that could not start, and measures the plan's 10-second limit as CPU time. Every 0.5.0 trajectory's final snapshot (10 trajectories, both models) was re-graded into a new score run: qwen b319 gains `plan.fields-preserved` (94.0% to 95.0%); deepseek c750 gains `scale.speed-1.1`; nothing else changes.
- **Open: the S6 speed rungs are noisy.** deepseek c750's `scale.speed-1.1` flipped from fail to pass on re-grading with no change to its timing code: the rungs compare wall time against the reference, best of three, and a delivery near a boundary passes or fails by chance. This fails the noise gate (VALIDATION §1). It is left for E3's capability card (PLAN-007 WP2.2), where the speed measurement moves to CPU time with a declared noise test, as one change with its own version.
- The ca69 deepseek note (S7 plans, grader failed, audit found no defect) is a snapshot whose earlier commands are broken (`addDependency is not defined`): the plan checks need valid plans of the earlier stages' stores and fail for that reason; the audit's plan unit ran on fresh stores only.
