## WP3.3 AP stockroom (commits `3dbf998`, `da280a3`)

- **Harness**: the `workspace-simulator` profile, a `simulator` tool relaying each command to the package's trusted simulator outside the sandbox, one process per command with a private state; the command log goes to the grader as `simulatorRef` (CODE.md §4a).
- **Package** `task-library/core/ap-stockroom` 0.1.0, a prototype with one environment (decision D7 by the plan's recommendation; not yet decided by the user): 40 days of four products with lead times, uncertain demand, promotions and capacity; ten dev seeds.
- **Anchors** per item, on the item's own draws: myopic (0), a forecast-driven policy tuned on 30 sampled futures per setting (1), and the hindsight bound (reported). Gap (strong − myopic)/(bound − myopic) 0.42–0.80; the strong policy stays below the bound on every item.
- **Verification**: self-calibration 71/71. The strong policy overflows the stockroom on six of ten items when random delays bunch its imports; recorded on the card, not hidden.

## WP3.4 VIS v2

- **Package** `task-library/core/vis-v2` 0.1.0: eight project-authored briefs (dashboard, editor, multi-step form, landing pages for two brands, a phone-first schedule, a catalogue table with filters, empty/error/loaded states), each with fixed content (`public/content.json`) and stated `data-role` hooks. Splits by brief: dev `landing-fernwood`, `dashboard`, `multistep`; validation the other five. The briefs await the user's review (D5).
- **Functional checks** executed, not read: `private/scenarios.mjs` drives each page in headless Chromium inside the sandbox, offline, at 1440×900 and 390×844 (clicks, inputs, filters, toggles, navigation, states, timing for the retry), and saves the screenshots the judges see. Every check traces to the brief or the instruction. Clicks fall back to the element's own `click()` when a decorative layer intercepts the pointer, so hit-testing is not a hidden requirement.
- **Anchors**: a hand-made reference page per brief and a plain anchor (the same markup with browser defaults); both pass every check. Mutants: 38 (vis-001's two false passes among them) each fail exactly their targeted checks; a missing page fails all. Self-calibration 62/62.
- **Rating**: `scripts/judge-tournament-v2.ts` (new): per brief, pages meet both anchors and other models' pages in both orders, judged from screenshots only (no source, so hidden text never reaches a judge; visible text addressed to judges is a defect by the judging skill); Bradley–Terry per brief with the plain anchor at 0 and the reference at 1 on the log-strength scale, uncapped; mean over briefs with a bootstrap interval over briefs; judge exam (reference vs plain on all eight briefs, degraded references on two), order flips, agreement between judges, and a 20-pair calibration form for the user. The suite executor runs it for v2 packages; vis-001 keeps the original tournament.
- **Offline check**: the tournament's dry run renders the 16 anchors and 8 degraded exam pages and counts at most 160 exam calls with the five default judges; judging itself is paid and waits for Phase 4.
- **Deviation**: the family rating is in the tournament's own report (`visual/tournament.md`); folding it into the v2 profile table comes with the first VIS run.
