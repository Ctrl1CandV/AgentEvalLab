# Peer Review: Math and Physics Reasoning Tasks

This review covers the structured reasoning tasks in the development task library. Check that each problem is self-contained, the expected answer can be recomputed independently, and the scoring contract accepts valid alternative reasoning or witnesses.

For numerical physics tasks, verify units, domain assumptions, tolerances, and boundary evidence separately. For constraint problems, verify minimality of any conflict set and validity of a full feasible witness. Do not treat a passing automated numeric checker as proof that every reasoning skill was measured.

Record the task version, reviewer, evidence examined, accepted alternatives, uncovered ambiguity, and any required follow-up. Do not claim general reasoning ability from a small synthetic task set.
